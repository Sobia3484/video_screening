# Exploratory data analysis: main dataset

Dataset: the main relevant dataset (all videos judged relevant). 1,309 videos (youtube 576, tiktok 733). Collection date used as the reference date: 2026-10-02.
All numbers in this report are generated by `scripts/run_eda.py` from the data; nothing is typed by hand.
Statistical tests treat videos as independent, although some creators contribute many videos, so p-values are optimistic; effect sizes and confidence intervals are therefore reported, and the robustness step repeats the key results with a cap on videos per creator.

## 0. Data quality checks

Checks run before any analysis, so that charts and tests are not built on broken data.

- 1,309 videos (YouTube 576, TikTok 733); duplicate video ids: 0.
- YouTube: missing likes 7.8%, comments 39.8%, followers 1.0%, description 6.9%, tags 23.3%, hashtags 74.1%.
- TikTok: missing likes 0.0%, comments 0.0%, followers 0.0%, description 100.0%, tags 100.0%, hashtags 12.7%.
- TikTok has no separate description or tags (the caption is stored as the title), so 100% missing there is by design, not a data problem.
- No impossible values found (negative counts, likes or comments above views, future upload dates).

Tables: `tables/00_data_quality.csv`, `tables/00_missingness_pct.csv`, `tables/00_extremes.csv`

*Note: Likes and comments can be hidden or disabled by the creator, so 'missing' there is a platform setting, not a data error. Shares and saves exist only on TikTok.*

## 1. Derived features

Columns created for the analysis (duration groups, rates, dates, age group, subtopic, detected language). They come from keyword rules and text, are not human-validated, and are saved in the features file.

- Age group in the text: YouTube: child 23%, infant and child 22%, newborn / neonatal 17%; TikTok: infant 32%, infant and child 24%, child 19%.
- Language detection method: langdetect 1221, too_short 88. Texts with fewer than 25 letters are labeled 'und' (undetermined).

## 3. Video duration

How long are the videos, and do the platforms differ?

- YouTube: median 177 s (3.0 min); middle half of videos between 110 and 327 s; shortest 16 s, longest 154 min; 7% are 60 seconds or shorter and 12% are longer than 10 minutes.
- TikTok: median 50 s (0.8 min); middle half of videos between 22 and 93 s; shortest 1 s, longest 38 min; 56% are 60 seconds or shorter and 0.3% are longer than 10 minutes.
- Duration: YouTube median 177 s vs TikTok median 50 s (difference +127 s, 95% CI +112 to +138); Mann-Whitney p < 0.001; Cliff's delta +0.72 (95% CI +0.68 to +0.76), large effect (positive = YouTube tends to be larger).

## 4. Views

How often are the videos watched, and how concentrated are the views?

- YouTube: median 14,671 views (middle half 2,298 to 92,575); maximum 15,470,275. 16.8% of videos have fewer than 1,000 views and 33 have a million or more. The 10 most viewed videos account for 42.7% of all views and the top 10% of videos for 81.2% (Gini 0.878).
- TikTok: median 6,902 views (middle half 979 to 85,505); maximum 108,309,046. 25.1% of videos have fewer than 1,000 views and 44 have a million or more. The 10 most viewed videos account for 57.7% of all views and the top 10% of videos for 91.0% (Gini 0.935).
- Views: YouTube median 14,671 vs TikTok median 6,902 (difference +7,769, 95% CI +3,114 to +13,241); Mann-Whitney p = 0.003; Cliff's delta +0.10 (95% CI +0.03 to +0.16), negligible effect (positive = YouTube tends to be larger).

## 5. Engagement

How much do viewers react? Rates are in percent of views and are computed only for videos with at least 100 views (rates of videos with very few views are unstable).

- YouTube like rate: median 0.70% (middle half 0.40% to 1.25%), available for 520 of 550 videos with at least 100 views.
- TikTok like rate: median 2.87% (middle half 1.54% to 4.78%), available for 721 of 721 videos with at least 100 views.
- YouTube comment rate: median 0.02% (middle half 0.01% to 0.06%), available for 340 of 550 videos with at least 100 views.
- TikTok comment rate: median 0.03% (middle half 0.00% to 0.11%), available for 721 of 721 videos with at least 100 views.
- Like rate: YouTube median 0.70% vs TikTok median 2.87% (difference -2.17%, 95% CI -2.43 to -1.92); Mann-Whitney p < 0.001; Cliff's delta -0.71 (95% CI -0.75 to -0.67), large effect (positive = YouTube tends to be larger).
- Comment rate: YouTube median 0.02% vs TikTok median 0.03% (difference -0.01%, 95% CI -0.02 to -0.01); Mann-Whitney p = 0.323; Cliff's delta -0.04 (95% CI -0.10 to +0.03), negligible effect (positive = YouTube tends to be larger).
- YouTube: Spearman correlation between log views and like rate rho = -0.15 (n = 520).
- TikTok: Spearman correlation between log views and like rate rho = +0.09 (n = 721).
- TikTok only: median share rate 0.22% and median save rate 0.51% of views.
